You can now play the Amazons. Sign in, open the new-settlement dialog on the play page and choose "Play the Amazons". You get a generated island and 35 Amazon building types, drawn with the original Amazon artwork that comes with the Amazons demo: the beekeeper's hut, the mead maker's works, the alchemist's den, the gem and sulfur mines, and the rest of the set.

We are fans, so we will say the plain thing first: this is a milestone, not a finished faction. Please read the next section before you judge it.

## What is real and what is placeholder

The buildings, their artwork and their footprints are the real thing, or as close as we can get from sources we can name. Construction costs come from the preserved manual. Footprints, doors and where goods are piled come from the reference remake we credit on every page that matters, the MIT-licensed work of Paul Wedeck and the people around it.

The timings are not real. How long a mead maker takes, how much honey a hive gives, how fast a gem miner digs: all of that is the same engineering placeholder the Romans started with, because nobody has measured it in the original game yet. The Amazons also have no divine interventions yet. We would rather tell you that than let you think the numbers are the original's.

## Why we trust the numbers only so far

We priced every building for all four civilizations twice, once from the manual and once from the reference remake, and put the two side by side. Of 153 buildings that either source names, 124 agree. 20 disagree, and 9 are named by only one of them: five are in the manual and not in the reference (the siege halls, the Gong Hall and the Gunpowder Maker's Hut), and the donkey farm is in the reference for every civilization and not in the manual. We do not pick a winner when they disagree. We use the manual's figure and keep both on record, until someone with the original game can settle it.

## The Egyptians and the Asians

Both exist as rules now: 32 Egyptian building types, with a brewery for kegs and a gem mine, and 33 Asian ones, with rice, liquor and a sulfur mine. Their economies run in our tests. But you cannot play them yet, and that is on purpose. The Amazons demo has the Amazon and Roman graphics and none of the Egyptian or Asian ones: 0 of the 36 Egyptian buildings and 0 of the 37 Asian ones. We will not draw Roman buildings and call them Egyptian. Those civilizations need the original game files for their art, and until we have them they stay in the tests.

## What is next

Computer opponents and skirmishes still play as the Romans, and a match between two different civilizations needs the engine to carry rules for each player, which it does not yet. The timings need measuring. The Egyptians and the Asians need their art. If you grew up with this game and know how long an Amazon bee really takes, we would love to hear from you.
